Privacy Policy — Lexoula (Greek Wordle)

Last updated: June 2026

Lexoula — Greek Wordle ("the App") is developed and operated by SmartBots.gr. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The App stores the following data locally on your device using the browser's localStorage:

  • Game statistics (number of games played, wins, current streak, best streak)
  • Today's game state (your guesses for the current day's word)

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

2. Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (property ID: G-QG42YT5TNN) to collect anonymous usage data, including:

  • Pages visited and time spent
  • General device and browser type
  • Anonymous interaction events (e.g. game started, game completed)

This data is anonymized and cannot be used to identify you personally. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Promotional Content

The App may display occasional promotional messages from SmartBots.gr. These messages do not use third-party tracking cookies and are served directly by the App.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Anonymous analytics data is processed by Google Analytics as described above.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

6. Data Retention

Game data stored locally on your device can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser or app cache. We do not retain personal data on our servers.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. If you have concerns, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
